Spent Solvents / Solvent Recovery Services

♻ Compliant Handling & Recovery  |  Hazard Class 3 (Flammable Liquids)

We provide compliant handling, sourcing, and recovery solutions for spent and secondary industrial solvents generated during chemical synthesis, pharmaceutical manufacturing, paint formulations, and industrial washing operations. By integrating recovery and recycling processes, we help manufacturing units reclaim value, reduce waste generation, and meet environmental compliance standards.

Common Spent Solvent Streams Handled
  • Alcohols: Spent Methanol, Isopropanol (IPA), Ethanol, Butanol
  • Ketones: Spent Acetone, Methyl Ethyl Ketone (MEK), Methyl Isobutyl Ketone (MIBK)
  • Aromatics & Hydrocarbons: Spent Toluene, Mixed Xylenes, Hexane, Cyclohexane
  • Esters: Spent Ethyl Acetate, Butyl Acetate
  • Chlorinated Solvents: Spent Dichloromethane (Methylene Dichloride / MDC)
  • Specialty Solvents: Spent Tetrahydrofuran (THF), Dimethylformamide (DMF), Acetonitrile
Services & Processing Capabilities
  • Custom Fractional Distillation: Reclaiming spent streams into high-purity, reusable technical-grade solvents meeting tight moisture and purity specifications.
  • Solvent Buyback & Sourcing: Procuring process off-cuts, mother liquors, and spent streams for reuse in secondary industries (paints, thinners, resin manufacturing, and degreasing).
  • Toll Distillation / Job-Work: Processing and purifying client-supplied spent solvents and returning high-recovery, recycled solvents for direct re-use in the client's plant.
  • Alternative Fuel / Co-Processing Supply: Channeling high-calorific, non-recoverable solvent waste for authorized co-processing in cement kilns in accordance with pollution control board norms.
Logistics & Regulatory Compliance
  • Authorized Handling: Strict adherence to Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, utilizing authorized manifest tracking systems.
  • Dedicated Fleet: Transport via GPS-tracked, spark-arrested, explosive-proof road tankers and certified closed-loop drum arrangements.
  • Documentation & Safety: Full traceability backed by Certificates of Analysis (COA), safety transport documentation (TREM Cards), and hazardous waste consignment manifests.
Safety, Handling & Storage

⚠ Hazard Class: Class 3 (Flammable Liquids)

Store strictly in cool, explosion-proof, covered hazardous storage sheds equipped with flame-proof lighting, earthing/bonding mechanisms, and secondary spillage containment bunds.

⚡ Operational Handling

Ground and bond all transfer lines to eliminate static discharge. Operators must use anti-static footwear, spark-proof tools, organic vapor respirators, and chemical-resistant nitrile gloves. Keep Class B/dry chemical powder fire extinguishers accessible at all times.
