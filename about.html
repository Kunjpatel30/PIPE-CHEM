PIPE CHEM — Trusted Chemical Supply Since 2004

Established in 2004, PIPE CHEM is a trusted chemical trading and supply firm committed to delivering quality industrial chemicals with reliability, integrity, and consistency. Over the years, we have built our reputation by understanding the requirements of our customers and ensuring dependable chemical supplies that support their day-to-day industrial operations.

With more than two decades of experience in the chemical trading sector, our journey has been guided by strong business ethics, transparent practices, customer satisfaction, and a commitment to long-term relationships. We believe that successful business is not built merely on transactions, but on trust, consistent service, and mutual prosperity.

Our Product Portfolio

PIPE CHEM offers a diverse range of industrial chemicals to meet the requirements of various industries and applications. Our product portfolio includes:

  • Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) – 30–32%
  • Dilute Sulphuric Acid – 70–74%
  • Dilute Nitric Acid – 24%
  • Sodium Sulphate – 93–99%
  • Phosphoric Acid
  • Sodium Hypochlorite
  • Ferric Alum
  • Speciality Chemicals & Performance Formulations
  • Spent Solvents / Solvent Recovery Services

We focus on maintaining consistent product quality, dependable sourcing, timely supply, and professional service, enabling our customers to focus on their core operations with confidence.

Expanding Our Capabilities

As our chemical supply operations grew, we recognized the importance of having a reliable transportation network to complement our supply capabilities.

🚚 MATANGI ENTERPRISE — Sister Concern (Est. 2019)

In 2019, we introduced MATANGI ENTERPRISE, dedicated to providing transportation and logistics support for the movement and supply of chemicals. The integration of PIPE CHEM's chemical trading expertise with MATANGI ENTERPRISE's transportation capabilities has strengthened our ability to provide a more coordinated and dependable supply chain — from sourcing to transportation and final delivery. Specialized Chemical Tankers — Transportation facilities include: MS Tankers: Mild Steel, offering strength and durability for compatible chemicals, HDPE Tankers: High-Density Polyethylene, offering excellent chemical and corrosion resistance.

This development marked an important milestone in our journey, allowing us to serve our customers with greater efficiency while maintaining our commitment to responsible and reliable chemical handling and transportation.
