Sodium Hypochlorite (NaOCl) – 10%–12%

💧 Industrial Grade / Water Treatment Grade  |  UN 1791

We supply industrial-grade Sodium Hypochlorite solution formulated to deliver consistent, active available chlorine (typically 10%–12% w/v). Recognized for its rapid biocidal and strong oxidative properties, it is an essential chemical agent for large-scale water disinfection, municipal effluent treatment, industrial bleaching, and institutional sanitation.

Major Industrial Applications
  • Water Disinfection & Purification: Widely used as a primary disinfectant in municipal drinking water networks, industrial cooling tower circuits, and commercial swimming pools to eliminate bacteria, viruses, and algae.
  • Wastewater & Effluent Treatment: Oxidizes organic contaminants, cyanide compounds, and sulfides; neutralizes offensive industrial odors and controls pathogenic loads in ETP/STP facilities.
  • Textile & Paper Bleaching: Serves as an effective bleaching agent for raw cotton fibers, cellulosic textiles, and recycled paper pulp.
  • Industrial CIP & Surface Sanitation: Used across dairy plants, food and beverage processing facilities, and healthcare institutions for cleaning-in-place (CIP) and microbial surface sanitization.
  • Chemical Synthesis: Functions as an oxidizing agent in organic intermediate reactions, chlorination processes, and hydrazine manufacture.
Safety, Handling & Storage

⚠ Hazard Details

UN 1791, Class 8 (Corrosive Liquid), Packing Group III

⚠ Handling

Causes severe chemical burns to skin and irreversible eye injury. Always use chemical splash goggles, full face shields, heavy-duty PVC/nitrile gloves, and rubber aprons. Never mix with acids, as toxic chlorine gas will be released immediately.

⚡ Storage

Sodium Hypochlorite naturally degrades over time due to heat, UV exposure, and transition metals. Store tightly capped in cool, dark, well-ventilated areas away from direct sunlight, organic materials, and acidic chemicals. Ensure containers feature pressure-relief vented caps.
