Dilute Nitric Acid (HNO₃) – 18% - 40%

🔬 Industrial / Technical Grade  |  UN 2031

Dilute Nitric Acid 18% - 40% is an aqueous, precision-diluted mineral acid solution with strong oxidizing and acidic properties. Prepared under controlled dilution to eliminate handling hazards associated with concentrated (68%) acid, it is widely utilized for metal pickling, passivating stainless steel, chemical synthesis, and industrial clean-in-place (CIP) operations.

Technical Specifications
Chemical Formula HNO₃
CAS Registry Number 7697-37-2
Assay / Concentration 18% - 40% (± 1.0%) w/w
Appearance Clear, colorless to faintly yellowish liquid
Odor Mildly pungent, characteristic acidic odor
Specific Gravity / Density ~1.140 – 1.150 g/cm³ at 20°C
pH < 1 (strongly acidic)
Boiling Point ~104°C – 106°C
Grade Industrial / Technical Grade
Key Applications
  • Stainless Steel Passivation & Pickling: Dissolves free iron surface contaminants and accelerates the formation of a protective passive chromium oxide layer on stainless steel fabrications, pipework, and vessels.
  • Metal Finishing & Etching: Used in aluminum deoxidizing, copper/brass bright dipping, and printed circuit board (PCB) chemical surface preparation.
  • Dairy & Food Processing CIP: Effectively dissolves scale, milkstone, and calcium phosphate deposits in automated pipework, pasteurizers, and processing tanks.
  • Fertilizer & Nitrate Manufacturing: Used as a raw material for formulating specialty liquid fertilizers and secondary nitrate salts.
  • Chemical Synthesis & Laboratory Use: Reagent for oxidation reactions, pH regulation, and intermediate manufacturing where controlled, moderate-strength acid is required.
Handling & Safety

⚠ Classification

UN 2031, Class 8 (Corrosive Liquid), Sub-risk: Oxidizer (mild at 24%), Packing Group II

⚠ Safety Protocols

Corrosive to skin, eyes, and mucous membranes. Vapors can cause respiratory irritation. Always handle using acid-resistant nitrile/PVC gloves, face shield, splash apron, and safety goggles.

⚡ Storage Guidelines

Store in a cool, well-ventilated, bunded area away from direct sunlight, combustible substances, reducing agents, alkalis, and organic materials. Keep containers securely closed with vented caps.
