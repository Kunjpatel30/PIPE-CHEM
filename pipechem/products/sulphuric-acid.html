Dilute Sulphuric Acid (H₂SO₄)

⚗ Industrial / Battery / Technical Grade  |  UN 2796

Dilute Sulphuric Acid is an aqueous solution of sulphuric acid, formulated to specific industrial concentrations (commonly ranging from 70% to 74%, or custom battery/commercial grades like 33%–38%). It provides a stable, highly reactive source of sulfate ions and strong acidity without the aggressive exotherm or extreme fuming risks associated with concentrated (98%) acid.

Key Applications
  • Lead-Acid Battery Electrolyte: High-purity dilute acid (typically 33%–38% / specific gravity ~1.28) serves as the primary electrolyte in automotive, tubular, and solar inverter batteries.
  • Effluent Treatment & Neutralization: Balances alkaline industrial wastewater, neutralizes process discharge, and manages pH in municipal and commercial ETP/STP units.
  • Metal Cleaning & Pickling: Removes oxidation layers, mill scale, and inorganic residues from copper, brass, and steel components prior to plating or finishing.
  • Chemical Synthesis & Fertilizer Manufacturing: Source of sulfate groups for intermediate production, aluminum sulfate (alum) processing, and chemical reagents.
  • Textile & Dye Processing: Used for fiber treatment, dyeing baths, pH adjustment, and washing stages in textile processing houses.
  • Demineralization & Water Purification: Regenerates cation exchange resin beds in industrial DM (demineralized) water systems.
Handling & Safety

⚠ Classification

UN 2796 (Sulphuric acid with not more than 51% acid), Class 8 (Corrosive Substance), Packing Group II

⚠ Safety Protocols

Highly corrosive to skin, eyes, and clothing. Always use chemical-resistant safety goggles, face shields, PVC/nitrile gloves, and acid-proof aprons during transfer.

⚡ Storage Guidelines

Keep containers tightly capped in a cool, well-ventilated, bunded area away from direct heat, alkaline compounds, and organic materials. Never add water directly to hot or concentrated residues; always maintain proper dilution protocols.
