Infrastructure & Capabilities

Built for Reliable Chemical Supply

Our operational infrastructure is purpose-built to ensure the safe, efficient, and compliant handling, storage, and transportation of industrial chemicals from source to delivery.

🏭

Dedicated Chemical Storage

Bunded, corrosion-resistant storage warehouses equipped with secondary containment systems, proper segregation zones, ventilation, and safety signage for safe long-term inventory management.

🚚

Integrated Logistics Fleet

MATANGI ENTERPRISE — our sister concern (Est. 2019) — operates a fleet of dedicated, compliant chemical transport tankers enabling coordinated, end-to-end supply chain control.

📦

Multi-Format Packaging

Capabilities to supply chemicals in a range of formats — from 25 kg bags to 1,000+ L IBC totes and bulk road tankers — tailored to customer operational requirements.

📊

Quality Management

Systematic quality verification including Certificate of Analysis (COA) validation, supplier quality documentation review, and batch verification for all products sourced and supplied.

📋

Regulatory Compliance

Full adherence to chemical transport regulations, hazardous goods handling standards, MSDS documentation, safety data management, and statutory requirements.

📞

Customer Support Operations

Dedicated sales, order management, and customer service capabilities ensuring prompt inquiry response, transparent pricing, and coordinated delivery scheduling.
