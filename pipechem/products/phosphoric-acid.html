Major Industrial Applications
  • Agriculture & Fertilizer Formulations: High P₂O₅ content makes it an ideal raw material for liquid NPK formulations, fertigation, foliar sprays, and phosphate salt synthesis (MAP/DAP).
  • Metal Treatment & Rust Passivation: Utilized in phosphate conversion coatings and pickling baths to convert iron oxides into protective iron phosphate, preventing corrosion and improving paint adhesion.
  • Industrial Water & Effluent Treatment: Controls mineral scale deposition, sequesters hard metal ions, and aids in pH adjustment for industrial wastewater streams.
  • Chemical Synthesis & Intermediate Manufacturing: Essential building block for manufacturing industrial phosphate salts, specialty flame retardants, and chemical process catalysts.
  • Descaling & Plant Maintenance: Formulated into specialized heavy-duty acid cleaners to dissolve hard water scale, rust, and calcium build-up in closed-loop systems.
Safety, Handling & Storage

⚠ Hazard Details

UN 1805, Class 8 (Corrosive Liquid), Packing Group III

⚠ Handling

Corrosive to skin, eyes, and respiratory passages. Handle with proper industrial PPE including acid-resistant gloves, protective safety goggles, full face shields, and chemical aprons.

⚡ Storage

Store in original, tightly sealed containers within a cool, dry, well-ventilated, and bunded area away from direct sunlight, reactive metals, and strong alkaline substances.
